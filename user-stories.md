# User Stories — Payment Management

## User Story 1

**As a student,**

**I want to make a school fee payment,**

**so that I can complete my registration.**

### Acceptance Criteria

**Given** a student has an outstanding school fee  
**When** the student makes a payment  
**Then** the system should record the payment

---

## User Story 2

**As a student,**

**I want to receive a payment confirmation,**

**so that I can verify that my payment was successful.**

### Acceptance Criteria

**Given** a student has made a successful payment  
**When** the payment is confirmed  
**Then** the system should generate a payment confirmation
